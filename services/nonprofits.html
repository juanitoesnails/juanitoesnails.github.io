Financial Literacy Programmes

Financial Knowledge
as an Essential Skill

Financial illiteracy is one of the most persistent barriers to economic mobility. Our financial literacy programmes are designed to bring practical financial education into accessible, community-centred formats.

Without financial literacy, communities are disproportionately affected by predatory lending, debt cycles, and a lack of financial tools. We change that by offering practical programmes that teach essential financial skills required to thrive in today's complex world.

Predatory lending
Vulnerable communities are at risk of falling victim to high-interest debt with unclear terms.

No savings safety net

Financial products misunderstood
Banks, loans, and insurance products often work against those who are most in need of credit.
◆
Debt cycles
Cycles of buy now pay later, credit card debt, and borrowed money commonly cause stress and reinforce inequality.
◆
Living paycheck to paycheck
Constant financial anxiety leads to negative mental-health outcomes.
◆
Lack of budgeting skills
Lacking budgeting skills creates significant challenges when managing income and expenses.
