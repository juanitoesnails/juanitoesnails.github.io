The Origin

Why We Built This

After years at the top of institutional finance, we started asking ourselves a question a lot of people ask mid-career: is this work actually helping anyone?

Our clients had always been large, anonymous, corporate. The kind where your work disappears into a system and you never see the human on the other side.

Then the people closest to us started asking for help. Not with portfolios worth millions — with the everyday financial questions that nobody had ever helped them answer. We watched something shift every time we sat down with them. Their financial worry became excitement and they felt confident about the future. This turned out to be the most meaningful work we had ever done.
